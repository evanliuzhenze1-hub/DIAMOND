COX-2 Selective NSAID

Dicelecox® Patient Safety Guide

Active Ingredient: Celecoxib 200 mg Capsules

⚠️ Important Safety Warning: Cardiovascular & Gastrointestinal Risks

Non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) like Celecoxib may increase the risk of serious cardiovascular thrombotic events. Use the lowest effective dose for the shortest duration consistent with individual patient treatment goals.

1. Administration & Food Guidelines

  • Food Requirement: Take Dicelecox® with food or a glass of milk to minimize potential stomach irritation or gastrointestinal upset.
  • Capsule Handling: Swallow whole with water. If swallowing is difficult, the capsule contents may be sprinkled onto a spoonful of cool applesauce and ingested immediately.
  • Missed Dose: Take it as soon as you remember. If it is almost time for your next dose, skip the missed dose and resume your regular schedule. Do not take extra medicine to make up for a missed dose.

2. Precautions & Allergies

Sulfonamide Allergy Warning

Do not take Dicelecox® if you have a known severe allergy to sulfonamides ("sulfa" drugs) or have experienced asthma, hives, or allergic-type reactions after taking aspirin or other NSAIDs.

Hypertension & Fluid Retention

Dicelecox® can lead to onset of new hypertension or worsening of pre-existing high blood pressure. Monitor your blood pressure regularly during therapy.

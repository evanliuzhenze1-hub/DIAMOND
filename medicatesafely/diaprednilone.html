Systemic Corticosteroid

Diaprednilone® Patient Safety Guide

Active Ingredient: Prednisolone 5 mg / 20 mg Tablets

🛑 MANDATORY WARNING: Do Not Stop Abruptly

NEVER DISCONTINUE DIAPREDNILONE® SUDDENLY. Abrupt withdrawal after prolonged use can trigger acute adrenal insufficiency, severe fatigue, hypotension, and body pain. Always follow your physician's step-down tapering schedule when ending treatment.

1. Optimal Administration Timing

Morning Dosing Rule

Take your prescribed daily dose in the morning (preferably before 9:00 AM) after breakfast. This mimics the body's natural diurnal cortisol release cycle and reduces insomnia.

Gastrointestinal Protection

Always take Diaprednilone® with food or meals to prevent gastric mucosal irritation or stomach distress.

2. Immunosuppression & Infection Risk

  • •
    Corticosteroids suppress the immune system, making you more susceptible to infections or masking early infection symptoms.
  • •
    Avoid close contact with individuals who have chickenpox, measles, or active viral infections.
  • •
    Inform your healthcare provider that you are taking Diaprednilone® before receiving any live vaccines.

2nd Generation Antihistamine

Diazenitine® Patient Safety Guide

Active Ingredient: Cetirizine Hydrochloride 10 mg Film-Coated Tablets

💤 Precaution: Sedation & Response Variability

Diazenitine® is a second-generation, low-sedating antihistamine. However, individual sensitivity varies. Exercise caution when driving, operating machinery, or performing tasks requiring full mental alertness until you determine how you react to the medication.

1. Indications & Dosing Guidelines

Adults & Children (Over 12 Years)

Take one 10 mg tablet once daily with a glass of water. May be taken with or without food.

Renal Dose Adjustment

Patients with moderate to severe renal impairment should consult their physician; a reduced dose (e.g., 5 mg daily) may be recommended.

2. Usage Safety & Interactions

  • •
    Alcohol & Sedatives: Concurrent use of alcohol or other central nervous system depressants may enhance drowsiness and impair alertness.
  • •
    Allergy Testing: Discontinue Diazenitine® at least 3 days prior to undergoing skin prick allergy testing, as antihistamines can suppress positive skin test reactions.
  • •
    Common Side Effects: Mild headache, dry mouth, fatigue, or pharyngitis. If severe dizziness or allergic reactions occur, seek medical assistance immediately.

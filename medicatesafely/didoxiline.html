Didoxiline® Patient Safety Guide

Active Ingredient: Doxycycline 100 mg Capsules / Tablets

🛑 MANDATORY POSTURE WARNING: Esophageal Ulceration Risk

DO NOT LIE DOWN for at least 30 minutes after taking Didoxiline®. Take each dose with a full glass of water (200 mL) while standing or sitting upright to prevent tablet entrapment and severe irritation or ulceration of the esophagus. Never take a dose right before bedtime.

1. Dietary Restrictions & Chelation Warnings

Dairy & Multivitamins (2-Hour Rule)

Calcium, iron, magnesium, aluminum, and antacids bind to Doxycycline, drastically reducing absorption. Do not consume milk, cheese, yogurt, or iron/calcium supplements within 2 hours before or after taking Didoxiline®.

Photosensitivity & Sunlight Alert

Didoxiline® causes heightened sensitivity to UV light. Avoid prolonged sun exposure, tanning beds, and wear broad-spectrum sunscreen (SPF 30+) and protective clothing while on treatment.

2. Usage & Administration

  • •
    Standard Adult Dosing: 100 mg every 12 hours on Day 1, followed by 100 mg once daily or 100 mg twice daily as directed by your physician.
  • •
    Take with food or a meal if stomach discomfort occurs, ensuring no high-calcium dairy items are included in that meal.
  • •
    Complete the full course prescribed to prevent bacterial resistance.

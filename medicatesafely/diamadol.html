Controlled Prescription Analgesic

Diamadol® Patient Safety Guide

Active Ingredient: Tramadol Hydrochloride 50 mg Capsules

⚠️ STRICT DAILY CEILING: 400 mg Maximum

NEVER EXCEED 400 mg (8 capsules) IN ANY 24-HOUR PERIOD. Exceeding the maximum recommended daily dose significantly increases the risk of severe central nervous system depression, respiratory failure, and seizures.

1. CNS Sedation & Alcohol Contraindications

🚫 Strict Alcohol Avoidance

Combining Diamadol® with alcohol or other CNS depressants (such as benzodiazepines or sleeping aids) can result in dangerous sedation, severe hypoventilation, coma, or fatal overdose.

🚗 Driving & Operating Machinery

Diamadol® causes somnolence, dizziness, and impaired motor reflexes. Do not drive, operate heavy machinery, or engage in hazardous activities while taking this medication.

2. Administration & Interval Timing

  • •
    Standard Adult Dosing: 50 mg to 100 mg every 4 to 6 hours as needed for moderate to severe pain.
  • •
    Swallow capsules whole with liquid. Do not open, chew, or crush capsules.
  • •
    Dependence & Tapering: Long-term use may lead to physical dependence. Do not stop taking Diamadol® abruptly without medical supervision to prevent withdrawal symptoms.

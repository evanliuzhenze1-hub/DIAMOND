Dilovirax® Patient Safety Guide

Active Ingredient: Aciclovir 400 mg Tablets | Oral Administration

💧 Critical Safety Priority: Hydration & Renal Protection

Aciclovir is excreted by the kidneys. To prevent renal tubular crystallization and renal impairment, maintain high fluid intake throughout the treatment period. Drink at least 2 to 3 liters of water daily while taking Dilovirax®.

1. Dosing Schedules & Course Completion

Herpes Zoster (Shingles) Regimen

Take one 800 mg dose (2 x 400 mg tablets) 5 times daily at approximately 4-hour intervals during waking hours (e.g., 7:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 3:00 PM, 7:00 PM, 11:00 PM).

Complete Course Requirement

The standard shingles regimen comprises a full 7-day treatment course requiring 70 tablets total. Do not discontinue early, even if lesions begin to crust or heal.

2. Administration Guidelines

  • •
    Swallow tablets whole with a full glass of water (250 mL).
  • •
    May be taken with or without food. Taking doses post-meal may reduce potential mild gastrointestinal upset.
  • •
    Missed Dose: Take as soon as remembered unless it is almost time for your next scheduled dose. Never double up doses.

3. Adverse Reactions & Monitoring

Common mild side effects include nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, abdominal pain, rash, or headache. Contact your doctor immediately if you experience decreased urine output, swelling in your lower limbs, or neurological changes (confusion, dizziness).

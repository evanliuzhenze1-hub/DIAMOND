Medical Affairs & Safety

Senior Pharmacovigilance Officer

Full-Time • Regional HQ (Malacca)

Lead adverse event tracking, signal detection, and regulatory compliance reporting across our regional portfolio including regulated analgesics and dermatological therapies.

📋 Core Responsibilities:

  • Evaluate and process expedited adverse drug reaction (ADR) reports.
  • Liaise with NPRA regulatory bodies and international health authorities.
  • Conduct Periodic Safety Update Reports (PSURs) and risk-benefit assessments.
  • Collaborate with quality assurance to maintain compliance audit readiness.

🎓 Required Qualifications:

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Pharmacy (B.Pharm/M.Pharm), Pharmacology, or Life Sciences.
  • Minimum 4 years of hands-on experience in pharmaceutical safety or regulatory affairs.
  • Deep knowledge of ICH GCP guidelines and local pharmacovigilance regulations.
  • Exceptional technical writing, analytical aptitude, and attention to detail.

Research & Development

Formulation R&D Chemist

Full-Time • R&D Laboratory Center

Drive the development and optimization of novel oral solid dosages, effervescent tablets, and advanced liquid suspension vehicles (such as sugar-free pediatric syrups).

📋 Core Responsibilities:

  • Design and execute pilot-scale experiments for sustained-release and fast-dissolve matrices.
  • Perform rigorous physicochemical characterization, HPLC assay validation, and stability testing.
  • Scale up formulations from laboratory bench to commercial manufacturing lines.
  • Prepare comprehensive technical transfer documentation and patent filings.

🎓 Required Qualifications:

  • Master’s or Ph.D. in Pharmaceutical Chemistry, Chemical Engineering, or Pharmaceutics.
  • 3+ years experience working with pharmaceutical excipients, granulation, and tablet compression.
  • Proficiency in HPLC, UV-Vis spectroscopy, and dissolution profile testing equipment.
  • Demonstrated track record of successful product formulation delivery in a GMP environment.

Quality & Compliance

Clinical Quality Assurance (QA) Manager

Full-Time • Regional Office

Oversee rigorous quality assurance audits across clinical trial sites, manufacturing plants, and supply chains to ensure uncompromising adherence to Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP).

📋 Core Responsibilities:

  • Plan and conduct routine internal and external GMP/GCP audits.
  • Review batch manufacturing records (BMRs) and release certificates for commercial batches.
  • Manage root cause analysis (RCA) and corrective and preventive action (CAPA) programs.
  • Train operational staff on quality protocols and compliance updates.

🎓 Required Qualifications:

  • Degree in Pharmacy, Microbiology, Chemistry, or equivalent scientific discipline.
  • Minimum 5 years in pharmaceutical quality assurance or quality control management.
  • Certified Quality Auditor (CQA) or equivalent professional certification preferred.
  • Uncompromising ethical standards and exceptional leadership skills.

Critical Prescription Safety Notices

Key guidelines for commonly prescribed DIAMOND pharma therapeutics.

Dilovirax® (Aciclovir) Antiviral

Hydration & Renal Safety Priority

  • Drink plenty of water throughout treatment to prevent renal crystallization.
  • Shingles treatment requires strict 5x daily intervals (every 4 hours while awake).
  • Complete the entire 70-tablet prescribed course.
Read Full Dilovirax Guide →
Didoxiline® (Doxycycline) Antibiotic

Esophageal & Sun Exposure Warnings

  • Swallow whole with a full glass of water.
  • DO NOT LIE DOWN for 30 minutes after taking to prevent throat irritation.
  • Avoid direct sunlight and dairy products within 2 hours of dosing.
Read Full Didoxiline Guide →
Diamadol® (Tramadol) Controlled ℞

Analgesic & Drowsiness Warnings

  • Maximum Dose: Never exceed 400 mg (8 capsules) in 24 hours.
  • Causes drowsiness—do not drive or operate machinery.
  • Avoid all alcohol while taking this medicine.
Read Full Diamadol Guide →
Dicelecox® (Celecoxib) COX-2 NSAID

Cardiovascular & GI Precaution

  • Take with food or milk to reduce potential stomach discomfort.
  • Use the lowest effective dose for the shortest prescribed duration.
  • Inform your doctor if you have a history of hypertension or heart conditions.
Read Full Dicelecox Guide →
Diaprednilone® (Prednisolone) Corticosteroid

Morning Dosing & Tapering Rule

  • Take in the morning with breakfast to align with natural cortisol rhythms.
  • DO NOT STOP ABRUPTLY: Taper down gradually under medical supervision.
  • Avoid live vaccines and contact with active infections during treatment.
Read Full Diaprednilone Guide →
Diazenitine® (Cetirizine) Antihistamine

Once-Daily Relief & Mild Sedation Precaution

  • Take once daily (10 mg) with or without food.
  • While non-drowsy for most, test your reaction before driving or operating machinery.
  • Avoid concurrent consumption of alcohol or sedative medications.

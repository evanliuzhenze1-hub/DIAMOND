Critical Prescription Safety Notices

Key guidelines for commonly prescribed DIAMOND pharma therapeutics.

Dilovirax® (Aciclovir) Antiviral

Hydration & Renal Safety Priority

  • Drink plenty of water throughout treatment to prevent renal crystallization.
  • Shingles treatment requires strict 5x daily intervals (every 4 hours while awake).
  • Complete the entire 70-tablet prescribed course.

Didoxiline® (Doxycycline) Antibiotic

Esophageal & Sun Exposure Warnings

  • Swallow whole with a full glass of water.
  • DO NOT LIE DOWN for 30 minutes after taking to prevent throat irritation.
  • Avoid direct sunlight and dairy products within 2 hours of dosing.

Diamadol® (Tramadol) Controlled ℞

Analgesic & Drowsiness Warnings

  • Maximum Dose: Never exceed 400 mg (8 capsules) in 24 hours.
  • Causes drowsiness—do not drive or operate machinery.
  • Avoid all alcohol while taking this medicine.
